Privacy Policy — Arrow Flow

Effective date: August 17, 2026

Arrow Flow is an arrow-escape puzzle game for Android developed by Shlomi Yahbes (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The short version: Arrow Flow has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. It does show banner advertising, and advertising involves third-party data collection — that is described in full below.

Advertising

The free version of Arrow Flow displays banner ads supplied by Google AdMob. There are no full-screen or rewarded ads. To serve, cap and measure those ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and similar device identifiers;
  • your IP address, and an approximate (city- or region-level) location inferred from it — the app does not request or use GPS or precise location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, and the app version;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether they were viewed or tapped, and basic fraud-prevention signals.

Depending on your consent and settings, ads may be personalized (selected using data about your interests) or non-personalized (selected using only coarse context, such as your general location and the content of the app). Non-personalized ads still involve a limited amount of data collection for frequency capping, reporting and fraud prevention.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form on first launch, provided through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), where you choose whether ads may be personalized. You can reopen that form at any time from the app’s Settings screen and change your choice.

Wherever you are, you can limit ad personalization on your device through Settings → Google → Ads, where Android lets you reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization.

Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects through AdMob. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app purchases

Arrow Flow offers an optional one-time Premium purchase that removes all ads permanently. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. To validate purchases and restore them if you reinstall the app, we use RevenueCat, which receives a randomly generated anonymous identifier, basic device information (device type, operating system, app version, locale), and your purchase history for this app. RevenueCat does not receive your name or payment details. See the RevenueCat privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

Once Premium is active, no ads are requested or displayed, and the advertising data described above is no longer collected.

Game progress

Your game progress (solved puzzles, daily streak, stars, settings) is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Notifications

If you turn them on, the app can schedule local reminder notifications for the daily puzzle and your streak. These are generated and scheduled entirely on your device by Android; no push service is used, no account is involved, and no data leaves your device for them. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings screen or in Android’s notification settings.

Children

Arrow Flow is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where required by law or by Google Play’s families policies, ads are served as non-personalized. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

Data deletion

All game data lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. To have the anonymous purchase-validation record held by RevenueCat deleted, contact us at the address below and we will handle the request. To reset or delete the advertising identifier used for ads, use Settings → Google → Ads on your device; to request deletion of data held by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
