Arrow Flow

A calm arrow-escape puzzle

The board is a tangle of arrows. Tap one and it slides out along its own path — unless another arrow crosses its way out. Untangle the whole board, one unhurried tap at a time.

One rule. That’s the whole game.

01

Tap an arrow. Its head leaves in a straight line, and the body follows the path behind it like a train on rails.

02

If another arrow sits on that straight way out, it bumps and stays put. Clear the blocker first.

03

Empty the board. There is no clock — take the minute you need to see the order.

Built to be quiet

  • A calm design

    Flat surfaces, thin lines, a soft dot lattice. Nothing flashes, nothing nags, nothing races you.

  • A puzzle every day

    One fresh daily puzzle, the same one for everybody. A few minutes, then get on with your day.

  • A streak worth keeping

    Solve the daily puzzle to extend your streak, with an optional reminder if you like being nudged.

  • No timers

    No countdown, no par time, no pressure to move before you have worked it out.

  • Never a full-screen ad

    The free game carries a single banner at the bottom. No interstitials, no rewarded videos, ever.

  • One-time Premium

    A single purchase removes the banner for good. No subscription, no ads after that — yours forever.
