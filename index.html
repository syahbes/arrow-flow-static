Arrow Flow

An arrow escape puzzle

The board is a tangle of arrows. Tap one and it slides out along its own path — unless another arrow crosses its way. Untangle the whole board, one unhurried tap at a time.

One rule. That’s the whole game.

Nothing to learn, everything to work out. The difficulty is in the order, never in the controls.

01

Tap an arrow

Its head leaves in a straight line, and the body follows the path behind it like a train on rails.

02

Blockers stay put

If another arrow sits on that way out, the tap bumps and nothing moves. Clear the blocker first.

03

Empty the board

There is no clock. Take the minute you need to see the order, then watch the whole thing unwind.

Built to be quiet

Flat surfaces, thin lines, a soft dot lattice. Nothing flashes, nothing nags, nothing races you.

  • A puzzle every day

    One fresh daily puzzle, the same one for everybody. A few minutes, then get on with your day.

  • A streak worth keeping

    Solve the daily to extend your streak, with an optional reminder if you like being nudged.

  • A campaign to work through

    Hand-built puzzles that grow with you, and stars for a solve that cost you nothing.

  • No timers

    No countdown, no par time, no pressure to move before you have worked it out.

  • Never a full-screen ad

    The free game carries a single banner at the bottom. No interstitials, no rewarded videos, ever.

  • One-time Premium

    A single purchase removes the banner for good. No subscription, no ads after that — yours forever.
